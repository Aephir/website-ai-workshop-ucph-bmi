# Consolidate Instructions

Review corrections and preferences logged in `memory/pending-updates.md` and propose exact wording for standing instructions or skills.

## Workflow

Read the queue fresh. Check each pending item against the bar of recurrence or a clearly important explicit decision. Propose one exact change at a time, including where it belongs, and ask for Accept / Reject / Edit wording. Apply only after confirmation, then update the item's status. Leave items that do not clear the bar pending.

Never overwrite the queue, delete resolved items, or claim an instruction was applied without actually applying it.
